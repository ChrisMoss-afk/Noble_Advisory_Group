Co-Founder and Advisor

Mark Beck, MBA

Transaction, Capital & Financial Strategy

Where his perspective comes from

Mark Beck brings more than 25 years of financial and management experience to Noble Advisory Group.

His career has been built in banking, spanning corporate lending and syndications, leveraged and mezzanine finance, trade finance, and cross-border transactions across one federal credit union, four Canadian banks, and one U.S. bank.

What Mark brings to Noble

Mark has also held a CFO position at an international functional water company and began his career in auditing and management accounting, giving him the financial operating depth that many transaction advisors lack.

Selected background

He is currently an Adjunct Professor of Finance at Trinity Western University in British Columbia, where he brings real-world deal experience into the classroom.

Mark holds a BA in Economics from the University of Alberta and an MBA from the Ivey Business School at Western University.
