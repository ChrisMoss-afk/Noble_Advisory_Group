Co-Founder and Advisor

Seyi Oyewumi, EMBA

Strategy, Diagnostics & Ecosystem

Where his perspective comes from

Seyi Oyewumi brings 20 years of experience helping organizations navigate complexity, build resilience, and position themselves for what comes next.

His career spans corporate strategy, ESG integration, and digital transformation across real estate, food and beverage, technology, and non-profit sectors. Most recently, he served as Director of Corporate Planning and Strategy and Sustainability at Brookfield Properties, leading strategic frameworks and ESG transformation across a $10B+ North American portfolio.

In 2023, Seyi founded Oyewumi Inc., a diagnostic-led advisory platform focused on business continuity, succession readiness, and operational transformation for SMEs.

What Seyi brings to Noble

His approach to Noble Advisory is grounded in applied research, structured diagnostics, and a deep understanding of what makes businesses transferable, not just valuable.

Selected background

Seyi serves on the Mount Royal University Foundation board, mentors through Plug and Play Tech Center, and teaches ESG and social entrepreneurship at the University of Calgary.

He holds an Executive MBA from Queen's University and undergraduate degrees in Science and Physical and Health Education.
