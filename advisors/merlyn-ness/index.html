Co-Founder and Advisor

Merlyn Ness, MBA

Entrepreneurship · Operations · Transition Support

Where his perspective comes from

Merlyn Ness is an instructor in Innovation and Entrepreneurship at the Donald School of Business at Red Deer Polytechnic, where he works directly with entrepreneurs, business owners, and applied research partnerships.

His academic work is grounded in practical advisory, supported by years helping owner-managed businesses think through their futures, build stronger operations, and prepare for what comes next.

What Merlyn brings to Noble

Merlyn understands how businesses work at the operator level — the decisions, the dependencies, and the things that never make it into a financial statement but matter enormously when ownership changes.

At Noble, he serves as the primary client-facing advisor: the person who sits with owners, asks the hard questions, and builds the trusted relationship that makes the work possible.
